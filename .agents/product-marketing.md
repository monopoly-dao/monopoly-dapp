# Settley Product Marketing Context

## One-Line Positioning

Settley is infrastructure for tokenized real assets, starting with real estate.

## What Settley Is Building

Settley helps eligible assets become easier to own, finance, collateralize, and settle onchain. The first wedge is property: investors can own part of a property or lend against one, while owners can raise money without selling the whole asset.

The broader vision is conditional liquidity infrastructure for tokenized real assets. Real estate is the first use case because it makes the ownership, collateral, documentation, and lending problems concrete.

## Audience Roles

- Asset owners: bring eligible assets onchain and raise money against them without forcing a full sale.
- Buyers/investors: own part of an asset with clearer rights, records, and documents.
- Lenders: provide stablecoins against real-asset collateral with agreed rates and repayment dates.
- Deployers/admins: package property or other asset inventory into compliant structures for buyers and lenders.

## Approved Plain-Language Patterns

- `Own part of a property.`
- `Lend against a property and accept the asset as collateral.`
- `Raise money without selling the whole asset.`
- `Bring eligible assets onchain.`
- `Infrastructure for tokenized real assets, starting with real estate.`
- `Buy ownership, fund a loan, or raise against collateral.`

## Technical Language That Is Allowed In Deeper Copy

- tokenized ownership
- asset-specific lending vault
- compliance checks
- collateral
- stablecoin settlement
- repayment date
- enforcement path
- onchain and offchain
- legal wrapper
- transfer restrictions

## Banned Or Risky Wording

- Do not describe Settley as only a `real-estate platform`.
- Do not use unexplained `LP` language in public UI.
- Do not use vague phrases like `asset-backed opportunities` unless the surrounding copy explains ownership or lending.
- Do not use abstract copy like `opened to capital`.
- Do not use `on-chain` or `off-chain`; use `onchain` and `offchain`.
