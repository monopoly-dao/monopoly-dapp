## About Settley

Settley is real-asset infrastructure, starting with property.

The platform helps owners raise capital from property, buyers own documented property stakes, and lenders fund property-backed opportunities. The user experience stays simple: browse a property, review the terms, understand the ownership record, and decide whether to participate.

Under the hood, Settley uses onchain records, structured legal wrappers, compliance checks, and settlement infrastructure. The point is not to make users think about infrastructure. The point is to make real property easier to structure, access, finance, and manage.

### Why property first?

Property is valuable, familiar, and hard to move. Owners often have capital locked inside assets they do not want to sell outright. Buyers often want exposure without purchasing the whole asset. Lenders want clearer collateral, repayment terms, and recovery paths.

Settley starts with property because it is the clearest wedge for proving the broader system: tokenized real assets with conditional liquidity, documented ownership, and structured settlement.

## Vision and Mission

### Vision

To make real assets easier to own, finance, and settle without hiding the legal and operational work that makes ownership real.

### Mission

Settley builds infrastructure for tokenized real assets, starting with property. We connect three sides of the market:

- owners who want to raise capital without selling the whole asset;
- buyers who want documented ownership stakes in real property;
- lenders who want to fund property-backed opportunities with clear terms.

## Core Values

### Legal substance before interface

A property should not go live until its structure, records, and eligibility requirements are clear.

### Simple surface, serious infrastructure

The user interface should feel understandable. The underlying system can still be rigorous, technical, and compliance-aware.

### Conditional liquidity, not false promises

Settley does not promise instant exits or guaranteed liquidity. Liquidity depends on asset quality, terms, lenders, buyers, compliance, and market demand.

### Clear records

Users should understand what they own, what they are funding, what documents exist, and what happens after each transaction.

## How It Works

### 1. List or browse

Owners list a property and set raise terms. Buyers and lenders browse available properties with clear participation terms.

### 2. Structure and verify

Settley prepares the ownership records, legal structure, and eligibility checks before a property goes live.

### 3. Own, fund, or raise

Buyers receive a documented ownership stake. Lenders fund property-backed positions where available. Owners access capital without selling the whole asset.

### 4. Track records and activity

Participants can view ownership records, transaction history, documents, and updates tied to each property.

## Key Features

### Property listings

Browse property opportunities with images, location, available participation terms, and property details.

### Property tokens

Buy tokens that represent a documented ownership stake in a structured property asset. Tokens are the technical representation; the user-facing purpose is ownership clarity.

### Ownership records

Settley connects user holdings to structured records, documents, and asset-level data.

### Property-backed funding

Owners can request capital against property-linked ownership. Lenders can review terms and fund opportunities where available.

### Compliance-aware access

Eligibility checks, jurisdiction rules, and required user verification protect the market from unsupported participation.

### Protocol and architecture

The protocol paper and technical architecture describe Settley's broader conditional liquidity infrastructure for tokenized real assets.

## Operational Flow

1. A property owner or asset deployer submits an asset.
2. Settley reviews the property, documents, ownership structure, and eligibility requirements.
3. The property is prepared for listing with clear records and participation terms.
4. Buyers review the property and buy property tokens where available.
5. Owners can raise capital through configured terms.
6. Lenders can fund property-backed opportunities where available.
7. Settley tracks ownership, transaction history, documents, and repayment or settlement activity.

## Tokenomics

This page should be replaced with **Property Tokens and Ownership Records**.

Settley is not currently positioning around a speculative platform token on the public docs site. The immediate user-facing object is the property token: a technical representation of a documented stake in a specific property structure.

### What property tokens represent

Property tokens represent participation in a structured property asset. The exact rights, restrictions, documents, and economics depend on the property and jurisdiction.

### What property tokens are not

Property tokens are not a promise of guaranteed yield, guaranteed liquidity, or guaranteed appreciation. Each property has its own terms and risks.

### Why tokens are used

Tokens make ownership records easier to track, transfer where permitted, and connect to settlement workflows. The token is the record layer; the property structure gives it meaning.
